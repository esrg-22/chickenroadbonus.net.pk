Cookie Policy

Summary of the purpose of the cookie policy

This Cookie Policy explains how chickenroadbonus.net.pk collects and uses cookies when you browse our site. Short version: we use cookies to keep things running smoothly, understand how visitors interact with our content about the chicken road game, and occasionally personalise your experience. Nothing shady. Just the standard stuff you’d expect from any modern website.

We wrote this policy to be transparent - not to bury you in legalese. If you’ve ever wondered why a site remembers your preferences or how analytics tools count page visits, this is where you get those answers.

What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files that get stored on your device when you visit a website. Your browser saves them, and on your next visit the site can read them back. That’s it. They’re not programs, they can’t run code, and they don’t dig through your files.

Some cookies disappear the moment you close your browser - those are called session cookies. Others stick around for days, months, or even a year or more. Those are persistent cookies. We use both types depending on what they’re needed for.

Third-party cookies are set by external services we’ve embedded on the site, like analytics platforms. We don’t control those directly, but we do list the main ones so you know what’s on your device.

How and why cookies are used

We use cookies for a few distinct reasons. Strictly necessary cookies keep the site functional - without them, pages won’t load correctly and your session state gets lost. You can’t really opt out of these without breaking the site.

Performance cookies - think Google Analytics - tell us which pages get traffic, where visitors drop off, and how long people spend reading our chicken road content. We use that data to improve the site, not to profile individuals.

Functional cookies remember small preferences. Did you dismiss a notification banner? A functional cookie handles that so we don’t pester you again. Targeting or advertising cookies may be set by third-party partners if we run any promotional content. We’ll always flag those separately.

Cookie retention periods vary. Session cookies expire when you close your tab. Persistent cookies on our site typically last between 30 days and 12 months. Third-party cookies follow their own providers’ schedules - check their respective policies for exact durations.

Your rights

You’ve got real choices here. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings menu - in Chrome it’s under Privacy and Security, in Firefox it’s under Privacy and History. Takes about 30 seconds.

You can also use our cookie consent banner when you first land on the site. Accept all, reject non-essential, or customise by category. Withdraw consent any time by clearing cookies in your browser or revisiting the consent settings.

Be aware: blocking strictly necessary cookies will likely break parts of the site. That’s a trade-off, not a threat - just worth knowing before you flip the switch.

Under GDPR and similar frameworks, you have the right to know what data cookies collect about you, request deletion, and object to processing for certain purposes. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Contact details

Questions about our cookie practices? Reach us directly at contact@chickenroadbonus.net.pk . We typically respond within 2 business days. If you’re asking about a specific third-party cookie you’ve spotted, include the cookie name and we’ll track down the source for you.
