Privacy Policy

Brief description of the purpose of the privacy policy

This Privacy Policy covers how chickenroadbonus.net.pk collects, processes, stores, and protects any personal information you share with us while visiting the site or engaging with our chicken road game content. We believe privacy isn’t a checkbox - it’s a commitment. So we’ve written this in plain language rather than hiding behind walls of legal jargon.

If you’re browsing our reviews, reading guides, or just checking out what the chicken road game is all about, some data gets collected automatically. This policy tells you exactly what that is and what happens to it.

Use of the information collected: to improve the site, optimize performance and analyze data

When you visit the site, we may collect technical data like your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, and pages visited. This happens automatically through analytics tools. We use Google Analytics - it’s probably the most widely deployed analytics platform on the web, and it gives us aggregate data about traffic patterns.

Why do we collect this? Honestly, to make the site better. If we see that a particular chicken road strategy article gets 3x more traffic than others, we know to produce more of that content. If a page has a high bounce rate, something’s off and we need to fix it.

We don’t sell your data. We don’t rent it. We don’t hand it to advertisers so they can follow you around the internet. Any data we hold is used strictly for site improvement and performance analysis.

If you contact us via email, we’ll keep your email address and the content of your message to respond and, if relevant, to follow up. We won’t add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Description of measures taken to protect personal data

We take data security seriously. The site runs on HTTPS, so data transmitted between your browser and our server is encrypted in transit. Our hosting provider maintains physical and network-level security for the servers themselves.

Access to any collected data is restricted to a small number of authorised team members who need it to do their jobs. We don’t store sensitive financial data - we’re an informational site, not a payment processor. Passwords aren’t applicable here since we don’t run user accounts.

We conduct periodic reviews of our data handling practices. If we ever discover a breach that affects your personal data, we’ll notify affected users and relevant authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law - typically 72 hours under GDPR.

No system is 100% bulletproof. But we do our best, and we update our practices as new threats emerge.

Use of cookies

We use cookies to support site functionality, measure performance, and remember user preferences. The full breakdown is in our Cookie Policy - including what types we use, how long they last, and how to opt out.

Short version: we use necessary cookies (can’t turn those off without breaking things), analytics cookies (Google Analytics, opt-out available), and occasionally functional cookies for preference storage. We don’t use intrusive tracking cookies that follow you across unrelated websites.

Users’ ability to view, modify or delete their personal information

You have rights over your data. Under GDPR and comparable frameworks, you can request access to whatever personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate data, request deletion, or object to certain types of processing.

To make a request, email us at contact@chickenroadbonus.net.pk with the subject line “Data Request.” We’ll verify your identity (just to make sure we’re not handing someone else’s data to a stranger) and respond within 30 days. Most requests are simpler than that and get handled faster.

If you’ve contacted us by email before and want that correspondence deleted, just ask. We’ll confirm once it’s done.

Contact information

Privacy questions, data requests, or concerns - send them to contact@chickenroadbonus.net.pk . We read every message and take these matters seriously.

Effective date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of January 1, 2026. We’ll update it as needed and revise the date at the top of the page whenever changes are made. Significant changes will be flagged clearly.
